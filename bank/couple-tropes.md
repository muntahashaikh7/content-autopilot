- F1 romantic-sweet: tasteful married-couple warmth (cuddles, cheek kiss, embraces) + gentle humor

- F3 comedy-exaggeration: one small thing escalates to ridiculous extreme (micro-aggression loop)

- Reverse-engineered-prompt method: shot-by-shot breakdown (hook, beats, camera, dialogue rhythm, payoff) → ORIGINAL replica prompt with our characters, original dialogue — never verbatim copy
